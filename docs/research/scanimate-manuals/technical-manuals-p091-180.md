### 1. ANIMATION OVER TIME / TRANSITIONS (rotation controller = digital "sequenced rotation" generator)
- p154 Rotation Controller block diagram (SCCS-B-100): a digital sine/cosine generator whose angle is stepped by a clock, giving rotation (and oscillation) over time. Up to 5 "sections" (Section selector 1-5), each with its own memory-address+control cards (J5-J12 on p157); p157 also has J14 clock 5-7, hinting at up to 7 sections.
- p154 MODE SWITCH table (6 modes): 1 OFF = clock gate inhibited; 2 INIT TO FINAL = final compare stops clock (angle sweeps from initial angle to final angle then holds); 3 ROTATION SEQUENCE = EOS (end of sequence) stops clock; 4 FINITE ROTATION (limited number of revolutions); 5 CONTINUOUS OSC (free-running); 6 FINITE OSC (limited number of oscillation cycles). Switches A/B/C enable/disable the finite-count and continuous options per mode.
- p154 Controls per section: INITIAL ANGLE LOAD, FINAL ANGLE LOAD, NO. REV LOAD, SECTION RESET (0 deg), DIREC REV (direction reverse), EXT START/STOP, manual start/stop switch. "NO REV storage 4 bit" and "NO REV counter 4 bit (16 count)" => 0-15 revolutions max.
- p154 Angle resolution: 7-bit memory address up/down counter = 128 steps per quadrant (512 steps/revolution); quadrant status flip-flops (sine sign / cosine sign) and decode 0 deg / 90 deg; initial and final angle compare registers (init/final status compare + count compare). Handwritten: "first compare is quadrant, 2nd compare is count".
- p154/161 RATE CLOCK: voltage-controlled oscillator (VCO), 5 rate clocks (one per section; pots 1-5, "x1 / x10" ranges noted by hand on p154), ext start/stop. VCO schematic p161 (SCCS-105B-1): 1439 op-amp integrator fed by FUNCTION IN + FREQUENCY, 9601 one-shot (0.033 uF temperature-stable cap), MC848D JK flip-flop and 7490 divide-by-10 -> outputs X1 and X10 (decade). So the rotation rate itself can be modulated by a "function in" (voltage-controlled rate = accelerating/decelerating rotation). HORIZ RESET gates the clock start (clock syncs to line).
- p156 Control card (SCCS-101D): rate clock in -> gated clock; 9601 one-shots 1.5 us for CLOCK OUT and DELAYED CLOCK OUT; START/STOP latches (7420 cross-coupled), manual clock; INIT UP/DN data in; up/down flip-flop; 7493 counters + 74L85 comparator count revolutions vs REVOL LOAD; "FINITE ROTATION INHIB IN"; outputs INIT/FINAL COMP, UP/DN, COS SIGN, SINE SIGN.
- p160 Sequence control (SCCS-104A): 7410 R-S latch from EOS (end of sequence), BOS, FINAL IN, INIT IN; 9602 10 us one-shots give INITIAL (pulse/level) and FINAL (pulse/level) -> chains one section into the next (rotation sequence). EOS/INIT/FINAL signals go "to sections 2-5 control cards" (p154).
- p154 Vertical sync tie: "VERT RESET" and "VERT RAMP RESET" go to the section-length decode and memory strobe (p154, p158) => ROM/D-A update is locked to the vertical ramp/frame ("update sine/cosine data at section change").
- p179 CVS-121-2 2x1 video switch: toggles between two video/camera channels on vertical reset (handwritten: "Horiz or Vert reset defined on front panel switch"), 9601 reset-out pulse; CH.1/CH.2 status levels.

### 2. RASTER / SCANLINES (monitor CRT + deflection amps, p164-176)
- p164/165 Monitor X-Y deflection amp (SM-122C): magnetic deflection yoke (BLU/RED/YEL/GRN leads) driven by complementary power amps from +/-48 V and +/-36 V rails; 0.5 ohm 25 W current-sense resistor in series with yoke ("F.B. RET" = feedback return) => CURRENT-feedback drive; two identical boards (horiz, vert), BNC inputs J75 (X), J76 (Y); OFFSET pot (5K) per axis; IC1 2515 op-amp; test points TP1-TP9.
- p165 Notes: "LIN 1 T.C. 100-600 us; LIN 2 T.C. 20-120 us; LIN 3 T.C. 0.5-3 us" (1A,2A,3A amplitudes) = three yoke-linearization/compensation time constants; also "omit these components for CAESAR system" (CAESAR is a sister product; SCANIMATE 1000 / CAESAR B 500B mentioned on p180).
- p174 CVS-128 Mon. Yoke Comp & Pincushion Adder: X and Y summing amps (40J op-amp) with R-C networks (5100 pF/1000 pF/120 pF with 20K and 100K trimmers: LIN 1, LIN 2, LIN 3, 1A, 2A, 3A) = frequency-dependent yoke compensation (boost of fast deflection edges), plus X-pinc / Y-pinc peaking caps (4-20 pF).
- p175 CVS-127 Mon. Pincushion Correction Generator: 4094 analog multipliers; IC2 squares X (TOP & BOTTOM correction pot 5K), IC4 squares Y (LEFT & RIGHT correction pot 5K), summed by a 2515 op-amp, then IC3 multiplies by X and IC5 by Y -> X and Y correction outputs (a cubic-type term ~ X*(aX^2+bY^2)); fed to the p174 adder. So the monitor raster is deliberately pre-distorted to compensate CRT/yoke geometry.
- p173 signal flow: X IN (J19-E) and Y IN (J19-K) -> pincushion gen (J3) -> yoke comp/adder (J4) -> X OUT (J32), Y OUT (J34) to deflection amps. (X/Y in come from the Scanimate deflection chain.)
- p171 SM-139A: INTENSITY COMP IN (0 to +10 V) multiplied into video via MC1595L analog multiplier (IC1) -> intensity compensation (handwritten: "Depth osc on ... zero video when size goes zero"; "flat black level"). Beam intensity is compensated for raster size/scan velocity so brightness stays constant when the picture is scaled; video goes to zero when size goes to zero.
- p171 Video out stage swings approx +12 to -34 V (handwritten) to CRT cathode/grid; BRIGHTNESS pot 1M on +70 V; CRT bias out; surge protect; HORIZ RESET IN generates blanking during retrace (7440 + Q8); BLANKING IN via 7440 gate.
- p171/172 Focus: focus-coil current regulator (LM305 + MPS-U56, 0.5 ohm sense, +36/48 V) feeding FOCUS COIL with a front-panel 50 ohm FOCUS pot (+50 ohm 5 W series): electromagnetic focus (coil). p169 front panel has INTENSITY (R1) and FOCUS (R2) pots only.
- p168 SM-124 HV supply: 20 kV CRT anode (voltage multiplier "A1" from 30 kV winding; 5000 pF 20 kV), +1000 V (500 V tap) supply, +70 V and +100 V DC, transformer + transistor oscillator, HV ADJ 250K pot, -11.7 V reference. (Handwritten "Deprecated" on p168.)

### 3. DEFLECTION / SIGNAL GENERATORS (as covered here)
- Rotation controller outputs SINE and COSINE, +/-10 V, from two 9-bit D/As (p154, p159) => drive the rotation channels. 7-bit address + sign => 8-bit magnitude (8x128-bit ROM, p154 "8x128 BIT R.O.M."), D/A with sign bit (9 bit). Sine/cosine ROM p158 (IC14 "MM 522 DBM"); D/A p159 uses two AIMDAC-100 converters, 74H87 true/complement, 7475 latches, output buffers with SINE OFFSET and GAIN trims, push-pull 2N3569/2N3644 outputs. Cosine address is the complemented sine address ("ADR COMPLEMENT ON COSINE UPDATE", p154): cos from the same table.
- p154: sine/cosine held in "sine storage"/"cosine storage" latches updated by memory strobe => stepwise (digital) angle; output is a staircase at each clock (128 steps/quadrant; 1.5 us strobe pulses p158).
- p158: IC15/IC16 (7493 + 74155) decode "SECTION 1-7 ENABLE"; 4 us and 1.5 us one-shots (9601) time the memory strobe/D-A clocks after vertical ramp reset (cycle: reset -> read ROM -> update sine D/A -> update cosine D/A).
- p161 "FUNCTION IN" on each rate-clock VCO lets other Scanimate signals modulate rotation speed.

### ROTATION CONTROLLER (pp.99-110; numbered "page (2)...(6)" in the manual) - HIGH VALUE
(Correct page map for pp.91-98: p91 SM-134B Animated Adder & Differentiator parts (cont); p92 SM-134B parts (caps, transistors, MC1439G, HA2-2515-5 op-amps); p93 SM-135 "X Animation & Differentiator" card parts; p94-95 SM-136A Signal Rotation card parts (MC1595L multiplier, 40J, HA2-2515, ICL8007, 2N4351 FET, DM8520N; 24 jacks); p95 also SM-138A Sequence card parts (SN7400, MC3029P); p96 handwritten "Connecting two LA computers" (10 lines A,B,C,H,M,R,P,N,J,D,E, VTR switch OFF/COMP, remote start, "other scan"); p97 blank gray; p98 Rack #1 circuit-breaker panel list C1-00057 incl. SM-137 50/60 Hz reference board.)
- p99 THEORY: system supplies voltages = sine and cosine of any angle, fed to "J11 signal rotation card" to ROTATE THE SCANIMATE RASTER. Max FIVE sections, each can have a different angle. Sine/cosine values are updated only at the BEGINNING of each section and held constant for the section-length time (so rotation steps per section, held per scan segment).
- p99 ROM = 128 x 8 bit holding sine 0-90 deg: resolution 90/128 = 0.7 deg; 8-bit accuracy 1 part in 256 (+/-0.4% of true value). 9-bit D/A, MSB = sign: all zeros = -10 V, all ones = +10 V, 100000000 = ~0 V. Output +/-10 V.
- p100-102 Operation (Fig 1, Fig 2): clock + RATE pot -> 7-bit up/down counter (0-127 = 0-90 deg) -> ROM -> data-complement net (controlled by FF2/MSB = sine sign) -> 9-bit D/A. FF1 sets count up/down; 90-deg gate resets FF1 (count down), 0-deg gate sets FF1 & complements FF2. Table: Q1 up, D/A 100000000->111111111; Q2 down, 111111111->100000000; Q3 up, 011111111->000000000; Q4 down, 000000000->011111111. Sine frequency set by clock rate. DIRECTION CHANGE button. p104 Cosine: complement the ROM address (read ROM top-to-bottom) = cosine of same angle.
- p103 MODE SWITCH (SCCSW-102): 4-pole 6-position rotary (JRC 7216, S22-S26 = sections 1-5); positions: 1 OFF, 2 INIT. TO FINAL, 3 ROTATION SEQUENCE, 4 FINITE OSCILLATION, 5 CONTINUOUS ROTATION, 6 FINITE ROTATION? (labelled on p103: 4 FINITE OSCILLATION, 5 CONTINUOUS ROTATION, 6 FINITE OSCILLATION as printed) - lines: OSC INHIB, FINITE ROT INHIB, OFF MODE, EOS bus.
- p105 OPERATING PROCEDURE: rotates raster about the "Z" (depth) axis; for animation sequences OR to make best use of raster space: lay artwork out horizontally (long vertical piece placed horizontally uses only a few scan lines instead of whole raster) then rotate into place. Up to 5 sections rotate independently; rate, direction (CW/CCW), mode differ per section.
- p105 Rate controls: ten-turn pot (ratio ~10:1, min = CCW, max = CW, i.e. center = stop-ish through bipolar), 3-position rate switch X1 / previous section / X10 (X10 multiplies by 10; center copies previous section's rate so several sections rotate at same rate), MANUAL button steps raster ~0.7 deg per press (works only when START/STOP is in STOP).
- p106 DIRECTION REVERSE button: reverses at any time, immediately if rotating, or sets the opposite of previous direction if stopped. SECTION SELECTOR switch picks which section INITIAL ANGLE LOAD / FINAL ANGLE LOAD / SECTION RESET / NUMBER OF REVOLUTION LOAD apply to. INITIAL ANGLE LOAD stores section angle and direction (stored when button RELEASED); raster section rotates to this angle whenever the Scanimate INITIAL/FINAL switch goes final->initial. FINAL ANGLE LOAD stores angle for init/final, finite rotation, continuous/finite oscillation. Do NOT load exactly 0 or 90 deg. SECTION RESET sets section to 0 deg, CW. REV LOAD: one revolution per push; max 15 (p107); increase anytime, decrease only after SECTION RESET.
- p107 INITIAL ANGLE SET button sets ALL sections to preset initial angles/directions and resets revolution counters, even in OFF mode (sequence set-up). AXIS / CENTER OF ROTATION is controlled by the horizontal and vertical axis COMMUTATORS; may lie inside or outside the section; can be moved only at 0 deg; when raster is rotated the axes rotate with it, so at 90/270 deg the H and V commutators swap functions.
- p108 MODES: OFF = rate clock disabled, section holds angle in initial-angle register (changes if INITIAL/FINAL switch toggled final->initial unless stored). INITIAL TO FINAL = rotates initial -> final angle in preset direction (angles must differ; for <360 deg total). ROTATION SEQUENCE = synchronizes rotation to the SEQUENCE RAMP in Scanimate: on actuating initial/final switch section starts rotating from stored initial angle; final angle = whatever angle the raster has when the electronic switch cuts in = end of the sequence ramp.
- p109 ROTATION SEQUENCE: may exceed 360 deg; limited by max rate and ramp length; with fast rotation + long ramps the final angle varies each run because of tolerances in the sequence ramp generator and electronic-switch threshold (analog imperfection!). FINITE ROTATION: from stored initial angle through 360 deg back to same initial angle N times (max 15), stops at initial position; final angle register must hold an angle not equal to initial (control purposes). Procedure: SECTION RESET, INITIAL ANGLE SET, then press rev-load N times.
- p110 CONTINUOUS OSCILLATION: rotate initial -> final preset angle, reverse back to initial, repeat until initial/final switch reset to initial; oscillation must be <360 deg; set final angle first; raster must be rotating in the direction it will be coming from. FINITE OSCILLATION: same plus rev counter. EXTERNAL CONTROL: start/stop of each section controllable externally by ground (stop) or +5 V (start) on the Raster Segment Blanking patch panel.

### ROTATION CONTROLLER SYSTEM OPERATION (pp.111-118)
- p111 External start/stop: INITIAL/FINAL switch on Scanimate set to initial resets each section to its initial angle; sections do not rotate when switch=final while that section's external input is at ground; +5 V => section rotates through its cycle; grounding stops it mid-cycle. Lets operator rotate each section at a different time.
- p112 Block-level: each section has own rate control, direction reverse pushbutton, start-stop switch, manual pushbutton, mode switch. Initial/final angle load, section reset, rev load affect only the section chosen by section selector; INITIAL ANGLE SET is common to all 5 sections. Each section has own rate clock, control card and memory address card; the memory card and D/A card are SHARED by all sections at the appropriate section time. Sequence control card shapes pulse/level signals from the Scanimate initial-final switch and end-of-sequence.
- p113 Data flow: clock -> clock gate (on/off by mode-dependent signals) -> up/down counter (7 bit, 0-127) steered by up-down flip-flop (= direction) ; address + sign flip-flops define current angle; sections are collector-OR'd but only one active at a time (section enable). 7-bit address latched in memory address storage register at SECTION CHANGE time, through address-complement gate to ROM; 8-bit sine + sign strobed into sine storage register -> 9-bit D/A -> +/-10 V; "update sine strobe" then generates "update cosine strobe" which complements the 7-bit address; ROM looks up cosine; to cosine D/A. Initial/final compare pulses: reverse direction in oscillation modes; final compare also stops clock in initial-to-final; initial compare counts revolutions in finite modes. Two 4-bit counters + comparator: one stores desired number of cycles, other counts; equal => stop clock.
- p114 Rate clock cards J13/J14 hold VCOs + digital divider; each VCO has a control line brought to the patch panel on the SEGMENT BLANKING controller; grounding it stops oscillation. Rate via front-panel pots; range switch picks VCO output (X10) or VCO/10 (X1). J13 has 4 VCOs (sections 1-4), J14 has section 5. Start/stop switch selects VCO clock (start) or manual pushbutton clock (stop). Manual button for setting angles and calibration. Mode OFF disables both clocks. Initial-final signal from J15: final->initial disables VCO clock; initial->final enables (like start-stop).
- p115 Rotation stopped by: 1 zero-volt on external VCO control line; 2 start-stop to stop; 3 initial/final to initial; 4 EOS via mode switch A (rotation sequence); 5 final compare via mode switch A (initial to final); 6 revolutions compare via mode switch B (finite modes). Clock steering network (on memory address card) routes clock to count-up or count-down inputs; counter can be reset to 0 or preset to initial count storage. Decode 0 deg = count all zeros; decode 90 deg = all ones (p115-116).
- p116 Decode 0 deg toggles sine-sign FF and sets up-down FF (count up); decode 90 toggles cosine-sign FF and resets up-down FF (count down). Section reset: three status FFs all set, counter reset, revolution counter reset = 0 deg. Initial angle set / initial-final to initial: presets counter + status FFs from initial storage registers. Initial angle load stores 3 status FFs + 7-bit count; direction also stored; makes no difference whether rotating when pressed.
- p117 Example: SECTION RESET then INITIAL ANGLE LOAD stores 0 deg, CCW. Final storage registers work similarly but cannot preset counter. Initial/final compare signals via mode switch C clock the up-down FF to reverse direction in oscillation modes. Example: finite rotation 5 revs: section reset, press rev load 5 times; raster does five revolutions each time, always stopping at initial angle.
- p118 Memory card J2: section-length decode (counter of section change pulses + dual 2-line-to-4-line decoder) enabling one section enable line at a time. At VERTICAL RESET the counter resets and section 1 enable goes true (section 1 data appears at memory address storage register); each SECTION CHANGE pulse enables next section (2..5). Section change pulse generates memory strobe pulse storing 7-bit address + sine/cos sign; update sine pulse gates address through complement gate to ROM; ROM output valid within ONE microsecond; D/A settles within FIVE microseconds to sine voltage.
=> So the rotation angle is updated once per raster-segment ("section") boundary within the frame, each section = a horizontal band of raster with its own angle (the "sections" are raster segments = groups of scan lines defined by the Raster Segment Blanking controller). Angle values reset at start of each vertical sweep.

- Intensity comp (pp.147-150): brightness ~ f(area) via square-root circuit and ~|d/dt| of animation so image density/brightness stays constant when raster is scaled/compressed; video goes to zero at zero size. IMPORTANT for emulation: when the raster shrinks, line spacing compresses but brightness boosted; when stretched, lines spread (visible gaps) and brightness is reduced.
